# Routing policy for tenant requests

Departments: maintenance, cleaning, rent_contracts, keys_locks, disturbances.

Priority urgent: water leak or flooding, no heating when outdoor temperature is below 10 degrees,
broken entrance door or lock to the building, fire or electrical risk, elevator stuck with people inside.
Urgent cases must be dispatched to the on-call contractor and the caretaker phoned.

Priority high: no hot water, broken fridge or stove, elevator out of service, pests.

Priority normal: everything else, for example dripping taps, squeaky doors, garden and common areas.

Rent disputes, payment plans, contract terminations and subletting go to rent_contracts and are
handled by a person. Disturbance complaints go to disturbances; never reveal who complained.

Duplicates: if an open case exists for the same apartment and the same problem, add the new
information to that case instead of creating a new one.
