# Requirements meeting - Andersson Redovisning AB

Date: 2026-10-05. Attendees: Karin Andersson (owner), Johan (office manager).

## Background
- Accounting firm in Uppsala, 14 employees, ~220 small-business clients.
- Shared inbox info@anderssonredovisning.se gets 60-90 client emails per day.
- Johan spends ~2 hours every morning reading, sorting and answering routine questions.

## What they want automated
- Read each incoming client email and decide what it is: routine question, document
  submission (receipts, invoices, bank statements), deadline question (moms, arbetsgivardeklaration,
  bokslut), request for a new service / quote, complaint, or other.
- Routine and deadline questions: answer using the firm's own rules and price list.
  Replies must be in Swedish unless the client wrote in English.
- Document submissions: register a case so the right accountant picks it up.
- Quotes, complaints, anything about tax advice for a specific situation, or anything unclear:
  hand over to a human with a short summary.

## Rules
- Nothing is sent to a client without Johan approving it for the first months.
- Never give individual tax advice or promise a deadline extension.
- Never share one client's information with another client.
- Only email clients at addresses that wrote to us.

## Systems
- Email: Microsoft 365. Accounting system: Fortnox. Case list currently a shared Excel sheet.

## Success
- Johan's morning email time goes from ~2 h to under 30 min.
- At least 70% of routine questions answered without Johan rewriting the reply.
- Zero emails sent without approval.
